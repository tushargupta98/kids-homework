## Sight words to read fast

Fry words are words we see **all the time** in books. When you can read them fast, reading gets easier!

This set has 24 words, like **brown**, **trouble**, **cool**.

## How to learn a sight word

1. **Look** at the word.
2. **Say** it out loud.
3. **Spell** it, one letter at a time.
4. **Use it** in a sentence of your own.

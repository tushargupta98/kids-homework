## A family of words

Some words are like a family living in one house. They all have the **same ending**. Only the **first sound** changes!

This is the **-et** family:

**v**et · **b**et · **p**et · **l**et · **n**et · **w**et · **j**et · **g**et

They all end in **-et**. Say them: vet, bet, pet, let!

## Another family

This is the **-at** family: 🐱 **c**at · 🎩 **h**at · 🦇 **b**at · 🐀 **r**at

Word families **rhyme**, just like in the Rhyming words lesson.

## Change the first sound

Take **pet**. Change the /p/ to /v/. Now it's **vet**! Change it to /j/: **jet**! ✈️

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  Keep the ending the same and swap the first sound. You can make lots of new words!
</Callout>

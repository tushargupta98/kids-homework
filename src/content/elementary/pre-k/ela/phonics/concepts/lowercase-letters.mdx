## Big and little partners

Every letter comes in two sizes: a **big** letter and a **little** letter. They are the same letter and make the same sound.

<AlphabetChart show="both" />

Some partners look alike, like **Cc**, **Oo** and **Ss**. Some look very different, like **Aa**, **Gg** and **Rr**.

## Most words use little letters

Most of the letters in a book are little letters. Grown-ups call them **lowercase** letters.

<AlphabetChart show="lower" />

<Callout type="remember" title="Watch out!">
  **b** and **d** are tricky twins. **b** has its tummy in front: b-b-ball. **d** has its tummy behind. **p** and **q** are twins too!
</Callout>

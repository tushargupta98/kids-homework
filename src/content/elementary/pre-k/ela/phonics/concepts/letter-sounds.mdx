## Letters make sounds

Every letter has a **name** and a **sound**. The letter **B** says /b/, like in **b**ee and **b**anana.

Tap a letter to hear a word that starts with it:

<AlphabetChart show="both" words />

## Listen for the first sound

Say the word slowly and listen to the very **first** sound.

🐍 **sssnake** starts with /s/. That's the letter **S**!

🐶 **d-d-dog** starts with /d/. That's the letter **D**!

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  Stretch the word out like a rubber band: mmmmoon. The first sound you hear is the first letter.
</Callout>

<Callout type="remember" title="Remember">
  **C** and **K** can make the same sound: **c**at and **k**ite both start with /k/.
</Callout>

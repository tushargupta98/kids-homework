## Words have beats

Words are made of parts called **syllables**. We can **clap** each part!

- 🐱 **cat**: one clap. 👏
- 🐯 **ti-ger**: two claps. 👏 👏
- 🍌 **ba-na-na**: three claps. 👏 👏 👏
- 🐊 **al-li-ga-tor**: four claps. 👏 👏 👏 👏

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  Put your hand under your chin and say the word. Each time your chin drops, that's one syllable!
</Callout>

<Callout type="try" title="Your turn">
  Clap your name! How many claps is it?
</Callout>

## What is a rhyme?

Words **rhyme** when they sound the same at the **end**.

🐱 c**at**, 🎩 h**at**, 🦇 b**at**. They all end in **-at**. They rhyme!

🐶 d**og** and 🐸 fr**og** rhyme too. They both end in **-og**.

## Does it rhyme?

🐝 **bee** and 🌳 **tree**? Yes! They both end in **-ee**.

🐱 **cat** and 🐶 **dog**? No. They end with different sounds.

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  Say both words out loud. Cover the first sound. Is the rest the same? Then they rhyme!
</Callout>

## nk says /ngk/

**nk** is like **ng** with a /k/ on the end: pi**nk**, si**nk**, ba**nk**, tha**nk**.

It comes at the end of a word, after a short vowel.

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  Say **sing**, then **sink**. Hear the /k/ pop at the end of sink?
</Callout>

## Word chains

Change **one** sound at a time to make a new word: **ink** → **rink** → **sink** → **pink**.

Try it with letter tiles or on paper. Spell the first word, then change just one letter.

<Callout type="remember" title="Remember">
  Practice for 10 to 15 minutes a day. A little every day works best!
</Callout>

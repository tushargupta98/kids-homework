## ck says /k/

The letters **c** and **k** together make one sound: /k/.

We use **ck** at the **end** of a short word, right after a **short vowel**: du**ck**, so**ck**, ba**ck**, sti**ck**.

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  Two letters, one sound: duck is /d/ /u/ /k/. That is three sounds!
</Callout>

## Word chains

Change **one** sound at a time to make a new word: **rock** → **sock** → **lock** → **block**.

Try it with letter tiles or on paper. Spell the first word, then change just one letter.

<Callout type="remember" title="Remember">
  Practice for 10 to 15 minutes a day. A little every day works best!
</Callout>

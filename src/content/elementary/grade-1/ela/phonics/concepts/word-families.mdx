## What is a word family?

A **word family** is a group of words with the **same ending**. The ending is called the **rime**. The first letter is the **onset**.

**v** + **et** = vet. **p** + **et** = pet. **j** + **et** = jet. They're all in the **-et** family!

## Read a new word

To read a word, say the first sound, then the ending, then blend them: **m** ... **op** ... **mop**!

Once you know one word in a family, you can read them all. If you can read **cat**, you can read **hat**, **bat**, **mat** and **sat**.

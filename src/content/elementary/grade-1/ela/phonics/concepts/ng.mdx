## ng says /ng/

**n** and **g** together make one sound at the end of a word: **/ng/**, like a bell: di**ng**!

si**ng**, ki**ng**, so**ng**, wi**ng**.

<WordChart lesson="ng" mark="ng" />

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  Say **sing** slowly. Is there a /g/ at the end? No! **ng** is one sound.
</Callout>

## Word chains

Change **one** sound at a time to make a new word: **sing** → **ring** → **wing** → **thing**.

Try it with letter tiles or on paper. Spell the first word, then change just one letter.

<Callout type="remember" title="Remember">
  Practice for 10 to 15 minutes a day. A little every day works best!
</Callout>

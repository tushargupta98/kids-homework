## A, O and U with double l

When **a** comes before **ll**, it makes a new sound, like in **all**: b**all**, c**all**, t**all**.

**oll** sounds like the o in **go**: r**oll**, p**oll**. **ull** sounds like the u in **put**: f**ull**, p**ull**, b**ull**.

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  Read **all** as one chunk. Then you can read b-all, c-all, f-all, sm-all!
</Callout>

## Word chains

Change **one** sound at a time to make a new word: **all** → **fall** → **mall** → **small**.

Try it with letter tiles or on paper. Spell the first word, then change just one letter.

<Callout type="remember" title="Remember">
  Practice for 10 to 15 minutes a day. A little every day works best!
</Callout>

## The double letter rule

In a short word with a short vowel, **f**, **l**, **s** and **z** at the end are usually doubled.

- o**ff** · be**ll** · me**ss** · ja**zz**

Two letters, but **one sound**: bell says /b/ /e/ /l/.

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  A few words break the rule: **bus**, **yes**, **gas** and **if** have just one letter. Most short words follow it!
</Callout>

## Word chains

Change **one** sound at a time to make a new word: **fell** → **tell** → **sell** → **spell**.

Try it with letter tiles or on paper. Spell the first word, then change just one letter.

<Callout type="remember" title="Remember">
  Practice for 10 to 15 minutes a day. A little every day works best!
</Callout>

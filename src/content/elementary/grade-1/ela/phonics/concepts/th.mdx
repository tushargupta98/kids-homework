## th says /th/

**t** and **h** together make the sound **/th/**. Put your tongue between your teeth and blow: **th**in, ba**th**, ma**th**, wi**th**.

<WordChart lesson="th" mark="th" />

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  If you feel air on your tongue, you are saying /th/ right!
</Callout>

## Word chains

Change **one** sound at a time to make a new word: **moth** → **math** → **path** → **bath**.

Try it with letter tiles or on paper. Spell the first word, then change just one letter.

<Callout type="remember" title="Remember">
  Practice for 10 to 15 minutes a day. A little every day works best!
</Callout>

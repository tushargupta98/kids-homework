## ch says /ch/

**c** and **h** together make the sound **/ch/**, like a train: ch-ch-ch! 🚂

**ch**ip, **ch**in, mu**ch**, lun**ch**.

<WordChart lesson="ch" mark="ch" />

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  Listen for the difference: **ch**ip starts with a quick, sharp sound. **sh**ip starts soft and long.
</Callout>

## Word chains

Change **one** sound at a time to make a new word: **chop** → **chip** → **chin** → **chick**.

Try it with letter tiles or on paper. Spell the first word, then change just one letter.

<Callout type="remember" title="Remember">
  Practice for 10 to 15 minutes a day. A little every day works best!
</Callout>

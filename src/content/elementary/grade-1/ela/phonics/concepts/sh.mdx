## sh says /sh/

**s** and **h** together make a brand-new sound: **/sh/**, the sound we make for "be quiet!" 🤫

It can come at the start or the end: **sh**op, **sh**ip, fi**sh**, wi**sh**.

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  Put your finger on your lips and say /sh/. That is the sound of **sh**!
</Callout>

## Word chains

Change **one** sound at a time to make a new word: **shin** → **ship** → **shop** → **shot**.

Try it with letter tiles or on paper. Spell the first word, then change just one letter.

<Callout type="remember" title="Remember">
  Practice for 10 to 15 minutes a day. A little every day works best!
</Callout>

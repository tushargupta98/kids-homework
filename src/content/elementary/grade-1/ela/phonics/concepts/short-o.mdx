## The short o sound

The letter **o** makes the short sound /o/ as in **o**ctopus.

When a word has **one vowel** with consonants around it, the vowel is usually short. These words have **blends** too: two consonants side by side where you hear **both** sounds, like **fl** in **fl**ag.

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  Read a blend slowly: f-l-a-g. Then say it fast: flag!
</Callout>

## Word chains

Change **one** sound at a time to make a new word: **cost** → **lost** → **loft** → **soft**.

Try it with letter tiles or on paper. Spell the first word, then change just one letter.

<Callout type="remember" title="Remember">
  Practice for 10 to 15 minutes a day. A little every day works best!
</Callout>

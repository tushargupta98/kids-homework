## What does it mean?

Every word has a **meaning**: what it is, or what it does.

- **duck**: a bird that swims and says quack. 🦆
- **jump**: to push off the ground into the air.
- **soft**: not hard, nice to touch.

## Check a meaning with a sentence

Not sure what a word means? Try it in a sentence!

"My kitten is **soft**." Yes, a kitten is nice to touch. That makes sense!

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  Some words are almost twins. **Fast** and **quick** mean nearly the same thing. You can use either one: "The fox is **quick**." "The fox is **fast**."
</Callout>

<Callout type="try" title="Your turn">
  Pick a word, like **lunch**. Can you tell a grown-up what it means without saying the word?
</Callout>

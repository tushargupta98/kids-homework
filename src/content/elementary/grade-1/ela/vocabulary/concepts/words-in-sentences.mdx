## Sentence clues

When a word is missing, the **other words** in the sentence are clues!

"The ___ swims in the pond."

What swims? What lives in a pond? A **duck**! 🦆

## How to find the missing word

1. **Read** the whole sentence, and say "blank" for the gap.
2. **Think**: what kind of word fits? A thing? Something you do? What it's like?
3. **Try** your word in the gap. Read the sentence again. Does it make sense?

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  If two words could fit, pick the one that makes the **most** sense. "I ___ water." Drink fits best!
</Callout>

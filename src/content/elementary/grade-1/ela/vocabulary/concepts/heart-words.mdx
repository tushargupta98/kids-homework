## What is a heart word?

Most words can be sounded out. But some words have a part that **doesn't follow the rules**. We have to learn that part **by heart** ❤️.

In **said**, the letters **ai** say /e/. That's the heart part. The **s** and **d** are easy!

<HeartWords />

## How to learn a heart word

1. **Read** the word and say it.
2. **Find the heart part.** It's in red.
3. **Spell** it out loud: s-a-i-d.
4. **Write** it, and say each letter as you write.

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  Some heart words are only tricky **for now**, like **go**, **she** and **look**. They'll be easy once you learn their spelling pattern!
</Callout>

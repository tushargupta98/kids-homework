## Words name things

Lots of words are names for things we can see. Look and say:

🐟 **fish** · 🦆 **duck** · 🔔 **bell** · 🚢 **ship** · ⛺ **tent** · 🦨 **skunk**

## Say it, then spell it

1. **Look** at the picture and say what it is.
2. **Stretch** the word out slowly: f-i-sh.
3. **Write** one sound at a time.

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  Some pictures could have two names. A 🧱 could be a block or a brick! Read all the words to find the one that's there.
</Callout>

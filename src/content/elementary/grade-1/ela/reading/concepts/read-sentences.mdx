## Reading a sentence

Now let's put the words together! Every sentence uses the sounds you've practised, plus a few **heart words**.

1. **Point** to each word as you read it.
2. If a word is tricky, **sound it out**: touch each sound, then blend.
3. When you get to the end, **read it again** so it sounds smooth, like talking.

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  A sentence starts with a **capital letter** and ends with a **.** or a **?** or a **!**. A **?** means it's a question, so make your voice go up at the end!
</Callout>

## Finish the sentence

In practice, you'll hear a whole sentence 🔊, but one word is missing. Listen carefully, then write the missing word.

<Callout type="try" title="Your turn">
  Read this to a grown-up: **She has a fish.** Now cover it and try to write it!
</Callout>

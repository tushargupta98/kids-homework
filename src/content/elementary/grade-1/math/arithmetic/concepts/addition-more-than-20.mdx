## Tens and ones

Big numbers are made of **tens** and **ones**. A ten is a bar of 10 little blocks.

The number **27** is 2 tens and 7 ones:

<TensOnes n={27} />

When we add big numbers, we write them in two columns: **tens** on the left and **ones** on the right.

## Add the ones, then the tens

Let's do **23 + 4**. First add the ones: 3 + 4 = 7. Then bring down the tens: 2.

Tap **Next step** to see it:

<CarryAddition a={23} b={4} />

So 23 + 4 = **27**.

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  Always start with the **ones**. Then add the tens.
</Callout>

## Too many ones? Carry!

Now let's do **27 + 5**. Add the ones: 7 + 5 = **12**.

But 12 is too big to fit in the ones place! 12 ones make **1 ten and 2 ones**. Look, the ones fill up a whole ten with 2 left over:

<TenFrame frames={2} filled={12} interactive={false} />

So we trade 10 ones for 1 ten. We write **2** in the ones place and **carry** a little **1** up to the top of the tens column. Then we add the tens: 1 + 2 = 3.

<CarryAddition a={27} b={5} />

So 27 + 5 = **32**.

<Callout type="remember" title="Remember">
  10 ones = 1 ten. When the ones make 10 or more, carry the 1 to the tens!
</Callout>

## Two big numbers

It works the same way with two big numbers. Let's do **38 + 24**.

1. Ones: 8 + 4 = 12. Write **2**, carry the **1**.
2. Tens: 1 + 3 + 2 = **6**.

<CarryAddition a={38} b={24} />

So 38 + 24 = **62**!

</Callout>

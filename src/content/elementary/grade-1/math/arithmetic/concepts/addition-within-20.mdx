## What is adding?

**Adding** means putting groups together to find out **how many in all**.

You have 3 apples. A friend gives you 2 more. How many apples now?

<Counters a={3} b={2} emoji="🍎" />

We write it like this: **3 + 2 = 5**. We say "three plus two equals five."

## Count on with a number line

Start at the **bigger** number. Then hop forward one step at a time.

Let's do **8 + 5**. Start at 8 and make 5 hops:

<NumberLine min={0} max={20} start={8} jump={5} />

You land on **13**. So 8 + 5 = 13!

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  Always start with the bigger number. It means fewer hops!
</Callout>

## Make a ten

Ten is a friendly number. For **9 + 4**, move 1 from the 4 to make 10. Then 10 + 3 = **13**.

Tap the boxes to fill the ten-frames and try it yourself:

<TenFrame frames={2} filled={9} />

<Callout type="remember" title="Remember">
  Switching the order doesn't change the answer: 4 + 9 is the same as 9 + 4.
</Callout>

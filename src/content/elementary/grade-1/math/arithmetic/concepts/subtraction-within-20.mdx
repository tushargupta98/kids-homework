## What is subtracting?

**Subtracting** means taking some away to find out **how many are left**.

You have 7 apples. You eat 3. How many apples are left?

<Counters a={7} b={3} op="-" emoji="🍎" />

We write it like this: **7 − 3 = 4**. We say "seven minus three equals four."

## Count back on a number line

Start at the **first** number. Then hop **back** one step at a time.

Let's do **13 − 5**. Start at 13 and make 5 hops back:

<NumberLine min={0} max={20} start={13} jump={-5} />

You land on **8**. So 13 − 5 = 8!

## Go back to ten

Ten is a friendly number for taking away too. For **13 − 5**:

1. First take away 3 to get to 10: 13 − 3 = **10**.
2. Then take away the other 2: 10 − 2 = **8**.

Here is 13 in ten-frames. Tap the dots to take 5 away:

<TenFrame frames={2} filled={13} />

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  Think of adding! If 8 + 5 = 13, then 13 − 5 = 8.
</Callout>

<Callout type="remember" title="Remember">
  In subtraction, order matters: 13 − 5 is not the same as 5 − 13. Start with the bigger number.
</Callout>

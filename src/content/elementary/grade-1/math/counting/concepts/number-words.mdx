## Numbers and number words

Every number has a **word**. Here are the first twenty:

- 1 one · 2 two · 3 three · 4 four · 5 five
- 6 six · 7 seven · 8 eight · 9 nine · 10 ten
- 11 eleven · 12 twelve · 13 thirteen · 14 fourteen · 15 fifteen
- 16 sixteen · 17 seventeen · 18 eighteen · 19 nineteen · 20 twenty

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  The **teen** numbers mean "and ten": four**teen** is 4 and 10, so it's 14. Don't mix it up with 41!
</Callout>

## The tens

20 twenty · 30 thirty · 40 forty · 50 fifty · 60 sixty · 70 seventy · 80 eighty · 90 ninety · 100 one hundred

For numbers in between, say the tens, then the ones: 47 is **forty-seven**. 115 is **one hundred fifteen**.

<Callout type="remember" title="Remember">
  **Forty** has no "u" — it's not "fourty"!
</Callout>

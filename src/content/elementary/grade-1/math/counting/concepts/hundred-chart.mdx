## Patterns on the chart

Each row has **10** numbers.

- One step **right** is **1 more**. One step **left** is **1 less**.
- One row **down** is **10 more**. One row **up** is **10 less**.

Tap 34, then the number just below it. Is it 44?

## Hop on a number line

You can hop by **1s** or by **10s**. Start at 23 and make 3 hops of 10: 33, 43, **53**.

<NumberLine min={20} max={55} start={23} jump={30} />

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  Hopping by 10s only changes the **tens** digit. 23 → 33 → 43 → 53!
</Callout>

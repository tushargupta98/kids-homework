## Count on

**Counting on** means saying the numbers in order, going up by 1 each time. You can start at **any** number!

Start at 47: **47, 48, 49, 50, 51…**

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  After a number ending in **9**, the tens go up by one and the ones start again at **0**: 29 → **30**, 59 → **60**.
</Callout>

## Past 100

Counting keeps going after 100! 98, 99, **100**, 101, 102… and on to **120**.

<NumberLine min={95} max={105} start={97} jump={5} />

Watch out for these tricky spots: 99 → **100**, and 109 → **110**.

## Count back

**Counting back** means going down by 1 each time: **32, 31, 30, 29, 28…**

<NumberLine min={25} max={35} start={32} jump={-4} />

<Callout type="remember" title="Remember">
  After a number ending in **0**, the next number back ends in **9**: 30 → **29**, 100 → **99**.
</Callout>

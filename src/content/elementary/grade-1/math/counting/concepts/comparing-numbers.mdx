## Three signs

- **>** means **greater than**: 52 > 47 ("52 is greater than 47")
- **\<** means **less than**: 47 \< 52 ("47 is less than 52")
- **=** means **equal to**: 30 = 30 ("30 is equal to 30")

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  The sign is a hungry alligator 🐊. Its open mouth always eats the **bigger** number!
</Callout>

## Compare the tens first

Which is greater, **47** or **52**? Look at the **tens** first.

<TensOnes n={47} />

<TensOnes n={52} />

47 has 4 tens and 52 has 5 tens. 5 tens is more, so **52 > 47**.

## Same tens? Compare the ones

Which is greater, **36** or **34**? They both have 3 tens, so look at the **ones**: 6 is more than 4, so **36 > 34**.

<Callout type="remember" title="Remember">
  36 and 63 use the same digits, but they're not the same! 36 is 3 tens, 63 is 6 tens. So **63 > 36**.
</Callout>

To put numbers from **least to greatest**, find the smallest first, then the next, then the biggest: **18, 45, 72**.

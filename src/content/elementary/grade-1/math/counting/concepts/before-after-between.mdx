## Just after

The number **just after** is **1 more**. Just after 59 comes **60**.

## Just before

The number **just before** is **1 less**. Just before 40 comes **39**.

## Between

The number **between** two numbers sits in the middle. Between 38 and 40 is **39**.

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  Say the numbers out loud in order. The one you say right after is "after", the one right before is "before"!
</Callout>

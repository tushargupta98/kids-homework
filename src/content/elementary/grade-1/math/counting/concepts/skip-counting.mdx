## Count by 10s

Counting by 10s is fast! **10, 20, 30, 40, 50…** all the way to 120.

On the chart, counting by 10s goes straight **down** a column:

<HundredChart max={120} step={10} />

You can count by 10s from any number. Start at 7: **7, 17, 27, 37…** The ones digit stays the same!

## Count by 5s

Count by 5s like the fingers on a hand: **5, 10, 15, 20, 25…**

<HundredChart max={100} step={5} />

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  When you count by 5s, every number ends in **5** or **0**.
</Callout>

## Count by 2s

Count by 2s by skipping a number each time: **2, 4, 6, 8, 10…**

<HundredChart max={50} step={2} />

<Callout type="remember" title="Remember">
  Skip counting is like hopping. Every hop is the same size!
</Callout>

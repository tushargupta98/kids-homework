## Count each one once

Touch each thing as you count it, and count each one only **once**. The last number you say tells **how many**.

<TenFrame frames={2} filled={13} />

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  Lots of things? Put them in groups of 5 or 10 first. Then count by 5s or 10s!
</Callout>

## Count by tens and ones

34 sticks are easier to count in bundles of ten: **3 bundles of ten and 4 ones** make **34**.

<TensOnes n={34} />

Count the tens: 10, 20, 30. Then count on the ones: 31, 32, 33, **34**.

## More, fewer, and how many more

🍎🍎🍎🍎🍎🍎🍎 | 🍐🍐🍐🍐

There are 7 apples and 4 pears. There are **more** apples and **fewer** pears.

How many **more** apples? Match each pear with an apple. **3** apples have no partner, so there are 3 more apples.

<Callout type="remember" title="Remember">
  "How many more" is the difference: 7 − 4 = 3.
</Callout>

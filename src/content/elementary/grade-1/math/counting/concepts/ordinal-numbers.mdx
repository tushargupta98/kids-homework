## What place?

**Ordinal numbers** tell the **place** or **order** of things, like in a line or a race.

👉 🐶 🐱 🐰 🐸 🐵

Start at the 👉. The 🐶 is **first**, the 🐱 is **second**, the 🐰 is **third**, the 🐸 is **fourth** and the 🐵 is **fifth**.

## Words and short ways to write them

- 1st first · 2nd second · 3rd third · 4th fourth · 5th fifth
- 6th sixth · 7th seventh · 8th eighth · 9th ninth · 10th tenth

<Callout type="tip" title="Hoot's trick">
  Look at the end of the word: fir**st** → 1**st**, seco**nd** → 2**nd**, thi**rd** → 3**rd**, and the rest end in **th**.
</Callout>
